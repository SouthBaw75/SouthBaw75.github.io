About

Hey — I'm Brian.

I live in South Louisiana and I make things. Mostly small web apps and games that started as an idea I couldn't stop turning over, and ended as something I could open in a browser.

Most of what's on this page got built in evenings and weekends. A few are genuinely finished. Several are prototypes I stopped touching the afternoon they finally worked. I've left both kinds up, because that's what building actually looks like, and pretending otherwise never helped anybody.

If something here is useful to you, take it. If you want to know how one of them works, ask — I'll tell you more than you wanted to know.
